Led Research Idea 5. Proposed the universal-embedding approach, drawing on the reading-week literature: extracted depth, segmentation and pose masks for each item, generated text descriptions of the frames and masks with other LLMs, and combined them with the RGB frames into a single multimodal embedding. Trained a scorer and spatial probes on these embeddings; gave the same combined inputs to a model fine-tuned only on spatial data and to its base model; and fine-tuned the embedding model itself on these inputs. Compared every setting against RGB-only, mismatched-mask, repeated-RGB and non-spatial controls. Also evaluated three zero-shot VLMs under the same inputs, four published spatial fine-tunes against their base models, and spatially reversed descriptions. Analysed abstention and error types, pooled the contrasts statistically, and compiled published spatial and social leaderboard scores across models. Ran a reinforcement-learning pilot and dropped it from the report. Set the updated research direction, wrote the Idea 5 section, and released the code and results in the team repository.
