End-to-end LoRA fine-tune of Qwen3-VL-Embedding-8B (r=16, alpha=32, language-model projections, vision frozen) on EgoNormia train+train_ext (600 items, 2 epochs, AdamW lr 1e-4, accumulation 8, tau 0.05; contrastive CE over the 5 action and 5 justification option embeddings). Inputs C2 (RGB + description), C5 (C2 + each depth/segmentation/pose mask followed by its description), X1 (C5 with another item's masks + descriptions), seeds 0/1, plus a zero-shot pass. Trained and evaluated on a Runpod A100 (scripts/emb_ft/emb_ft.py, lanes.sh); test labels never left this machine; scored locally by scripts/emb_ft/score_emb_ft.py -> scored.json. Primary metric: joint accuracy, argmax over non-empty options.
