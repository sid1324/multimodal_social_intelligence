Our first analysis examines the premise behind progressive cross-modal norm supervision: whether perceptual observations improve normative decisions and whether the improvement survives answer-text predictability. Using released EgoNormia predictions and direct audits of the proposed training resources, we compare matched input conditions, inspect norm-specific differences, and test linguistic shortcuts. We also examine contextual language and visual representations. These analyses refine the curriculum's controls and data preparation; they do not yet establish that sequential training transfers better than an equal-composition mixture.
